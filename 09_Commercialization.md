# Phase 9 — Commercialization Plan

## Manufacturing and supply chain roadmap

EVT uses controlled prototypes to prove measurement; DVT selects the EMS, qualifies BOM alternates and builds production fixtures; PVT validates pilot-line yield, packaging and service spares; commercial launch uses forecasted purchase orders, dual-qualified critical components, controlled substitutions and a warranty reserve.

## Go-to-market

Start in one or two cities with trained electricians and a narrow set of approved panel types. Sell an installed monitoring outcome with clear exclusions, never an unmanaged DIY panel kit. Use 50–100 paid or discounted pilots to validate installation time, willingness to pay, reliability and insight usefulness before scaling demand generation.

| Lever | Decision |
|---|---|
| Pricing | Test hardware-only and installed package separately; do not conceal installation cost |
| Distribution | Installer-led channel plus direct lead generation |
| Warranty/service | Publish coverage, exclusions, replacement process and electrician escalation |
| Support | Track first-contact resolution, RMA, repeat truck roll, cloud cost and CSAT |
| Inventory | Keep controlled builds; do not finance wide inventory before field yield stabilises |

## Unit economics and milestones

Track landed COGS, channel/installer payment, warranty reserve, shipping, payment fees, cloud/device/month, support/device/month, CAC and refund rate separately. A hardware gross margin without warranty, support and cloud is not a viable gross margin. Fundable milestones are: 20 safe engineering installs, 100 qualified pilot installs, verified accuracy/uptime, repeatable installation, paid conversion and a PVT yield plan.

## Hiring and fundraising

Core team: product/field operations, power/electronics, embedded, backend/mobile, QA/compliance and supply chain. Add data science only after quality telemetry and labelled field problems exist. Fundraise against evidence: panel-fit, safety path, unit cost, installation time, reliability, paid demand and regulatory plan—not a generic AI narrative.

## Strategic boundaries

Utility/DISCOM contracts, insurance, demand response, load switching, enterprise hospital/industrial deployments and white-label projects each require a separate compliance, liability, sales-cycle and support review. None is a prerequisite for Home-8 launch.
