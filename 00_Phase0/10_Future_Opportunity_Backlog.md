# Future Opportunity Backlog

## Rules for evaluating opportunities

An opportunity becomes a roadmap commitment only after a named customer, validated data source, safety/regulatory assessment, unit economics, privacy impact assessment, success metric and accountable owner exist. Interesting is not sufficient.

| Opportunity | Customer value hypothesis | Measurement/data dependency | Key risk | Earliest stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity reliability/usage profile | Landlord or lender sees stable, consented energy evidence | Longitudinal authorised site data | Discrimination, credit regulation and weak causal value | Research |
| Appliance health passport | Service history and energy trend support maintenance | Direct circuit/plug data plus technician labels | Misdiagnosis/liability | Phase 3 |
| Insurance-ready report | Evidence of monitored operating patterns | Insurer partnership and data consent | Regulatory, adverse customer outcome | Partnership research |
| Landlord/tenant transparency | Time-stamped circuit/submeter evidence | Correct tenancy/site permissions | Dispute/legal context | Phase 2 |
| Society-level anonymous benchmark | Compare similar homes | Large, privacy-protected cohort | Re-identification and misleading comparison | Phase 3 |
| Predictive-maintenance subscription | Managed alert/service outcome | High-quality labelled faults | False positives and field-service cost | Phase 3 |
| Saving challenges/rewards | Improve retention through voluntary goals | Reliable savings normalisation | Incentive fraud / dark patterns | Phase 2 |
| Solar, battery and EV optimisation | Lower import/capacity cost | Bi-directional/solar/EV measurements and control safety case | Incorrect control and tariff complexity | V2+ |
| Open developer API | Integrators build automation | Stable tenant-scoped data contract | Security/support burden | V2+ |

## Research backlog

NILM, energy score, carbon estimate, predictive life and bill verification are research/analytics candidates, not proof that a device can identify a particular appliance or diagnose an electrical fault. Each candidate requires a model card and evidence gate in the AI System SRS.
