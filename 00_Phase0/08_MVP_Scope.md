# MVP Scope

## Included

| Area | V1 commitment |
|---|---|
| Hardware | Single-phase monitor, one service-live CT input, eight branch-live CT inputs, isolated voltage reference, Wi-Fi/BLE commissioning, local status LED, isolated power |
| Measurement | Active energy/power, voltage, current, frequency, PF at mains; energy/power per labelled branch; target ±1% system energy in stated envelope |
| Firmware | Signed OTA, local buffering, calibration record, health telemetry, recoverable provisioning |
| App | Onboarding, circuit labels, live view, history, tariff estimate, alerts, export, privacy/delete request |
| Cloud | Tenant/device registry, MQTT ingest, 5-minute history, rules, notifications, support audit |
| Operations | Installer SOP, remote support, RMA process, pilot dashboard |

## Excluded

Three-phase, certified utility billing, remote disconnect/control, demand response, solar DC measurement, automatic appliance identification, payment/recharge, open third-party integrations, enterprise SSO, and high-resolution waveform retention as a normal feature.

## Timeline and resourcing

| Stage | Duration | Exit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Discovery + architecture | 6 weeks | Panel survey, product requirements, component risk register |
| EVT | 10 weeks | 20 engineering units, basic metrology and safety bench results |
| DVT | 12 weeks | Design verification, enclosure/EMI pre-scan, security tests |
| Controlled pilot | 12–16 weeks | 50–100 qualified installations, accuracy/uptime/alert evidence |
| PVT + launch readiness | 10–14 weeks | Production fixture validation, compliance plan/results, support readiness |

Indicative MVP programme cost: ₹0.9–1.8 crore excluding mass-manufacturing working capital, subject to team model, certification classification and pilot geography. This is a planning range, not a funding request or vendor quote.

## MVP success criteria

The MVP is accepted only when requirements in the SRS are met, pilot safety incidents attributable to product/install are zero, metrology and uptime targets in the Project Overview are achieved, and at least one customer segment shows repeatable willingness to pay for installed monitoring.
