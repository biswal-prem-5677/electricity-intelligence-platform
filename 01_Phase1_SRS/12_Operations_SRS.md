# Operations SRS

## Purpose and scope

Defines the people and processes that make installed monitoring safe, supportable and financially sustainable.

## Operating model

Customer support triages account/app issues; installer support handles site/installation issues; fleet operations owns device health/OTA; security owns incidents; quality owns RMA/CAPA; product owns customer communication and roadmap decisions.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| OPS-001 | Certify installers and maintain eligibility/insurance/training records. | Activation blocked for expired or unapproved installer. |
| OPS-002 | Use installation, site-rejection and handover checklists. | Pilot audit finds complete records for every activated site. |
| OPS-003 | Define support triage, escalation and customer communication. | Tabletop incident meets response/ownership goals. |
| OPS-004 | Track fleet health, RMA reasons, warranty cost and CAPA. | Monthly review identifies top failure modes and action owner. |
| OPS-005 | Manage data requests, retention and account closure. | Request workflow has auditable completion and exception path. |
| OPS-006 | Publish service limits and “call electrician” escalation wording. | Support scripts never provide unsafe mains-work instruction. |

## Non-functional requirements

Pilot support targets: acknowledge P1 safety/security reports immediately through staffed escalation; normal support response within one business day. Final SLA, warranty duration and spare policy are commercial decisions signed before launch.

## Future extensions

24×7 enterprise operations, field-service network and managed energy service are funded only after pilot demand and unit economics justify them.
