# future/V3 — The Fleet-Dependent Horizon

**Status:** Parked. Requires [V2.md](V2.md) delivered and a fleet producing data.
**What distinguishes V3 from V2:** every item here needs something we cannot buy, hire or design our way to. It needs **accumulated measured data from real installations**.

---

## 1. The precondition that governs everything here

A fleet of devices measuring real Indian circuits, at known sites, with installer-verified channel mapping, calibration versioning and honest data-gap handling.

That dataset cannot be bought, scraped or simulated. It is produced only by shipping V1 and keeping its data clean. **This is why the AI roadmap is, one layer down, a hardware and metrology discipline roadmap.**

Rough scale required: **≥500 devices, ≥12 months, across ≥3 site archetypes.** Below that, cohort statistics are anecdotes with error bars.

## 3. Standing constraints — these do not relax with model sophistication

1. **Explainability is not optional.** Every output states its evidence window, its rule or model version, and its limitation.
2. **A model may never imply a safety conclusion.** The boundary is architectural (F6, R21) and does not move because a model became confident.
3. **No claim without an evaluation dataset.** An impressive demo is not evidence (R13).
4. **Suppress rather than guess** (R15). MET-005 applies to models exactly as it applies to rules.
5. **Cost per inference is a product constraint**, like telemetry cost (D-004). A capability that cannot pay for its own compute is not ready.

## 4. The Switch-16 caveat

It appears in this list because customers will ask for it, not because it is a good idea. Adding a switching path reverses **F6**, the decision that removed the project's highest-consequence failure mode and a large slice of certification scope.

**If it is ever taken up, it is a separate product with a separate safety case, a separate certification, separate insurance and probably a separate liability structure.** It must never be a variant of a monitoring device — the failure mode is a customer who believes their monitor can protect them.

## 5. The honest position

Most of this list will not be built by this company, and that is a normal outcome. The purpose of writing it down is so that the V1 team knows what the data they are collecting is *worth* — and treats data quality as a strategic asset rather than a logging detail.
