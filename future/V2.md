# future/V2 — The Next Horizon

**Status:** Parked. Unparks only after **Gate 6** and the five conditions in [README.md](README.md).
**Character of this list:** everything here has a **concrete trigger** and could start within weeks of Gate 6. It is the shortest queue in the folder, deliberately.

## 1. What qualifies as V2

Three tests. A capability belongs here only if it passes all three:

1. **The trigger is observable** — a number we will actually see, not a judgement call.
2. **It reuses V1's foundation** — the same firmware, cloud, app and manufacturing line, or a clearly bounded extension of them.
3. **It does not require a dataset we do not yet have.** Anything waiting on accumulated fleet data is [V3](V3.md), not V2.

## 3. The sequencing argument

Items 1–3 are **cheap and defensive** — they extend the product without changing what it is. Items 4–5 are **expensive and expansionary** — each is effectively a new hardware programme with its own EVT, DVT and certification scope.

**Do not start item 4 or 5 until items 1–3 have shipped and the fleet is stable.** The most common failure at this stage is a company that has proven one product and immediately starts three.

## 4. Site-3P deserves a specific warning

It looks like a firmware feature and is not. V1 uses a single shared isolated voltage reference (F4). Branches on other phases sit 120° away from that reference, so active power and power factor are wrong for those branches. **There is no firmware workaround for a missing voltage reference.**

Treat Site-3P as a new hardware platform with a new certification scope, and budget it as one.

## 5. What must be true before any of this starts

- Gate 6 passed with verified customer payback

- Support load per site sustainable at the modelled cost
- A named owner with capacity, for each item taken up
- A logged decision naming what each item delays (R9)

**If the team is still firefighting V1 field issues, none of this starts.** A stable installed base is the precondition for everything in this file.
