# future/Moonshots

**Status:** Speculative. No trigger, no owner, no estimate, no gate.
**Rule of use:** nothing in this file may be cited in a plan, a pitch, a specification or a decision. It exists so that big ideas have a home that is not the backlog.

---

## Why keep this file at all

Ideas that are genuinely large tend to leak into planning documents disguised as features, where they distort scope and timelines. Giving them an explicit, clearly-labelled dead end is cheaper than repeatedly arguing them down.

**Reading rule:** if an argument from this file starts to sound like a reason to change V1, that is the signal that the governing rule is being tested — not that the idea has matured.

---

## The list

| Idea | What it would be | What would have to change |
|---|---|---|
| **The measured-energy layer for Indian small business** | Enough installed base that segment-level energy behaviour becomes a data product in its own right — benchmarking, sector reports, efficiency financing | Tens of thousands of devices, and a company that has learned to sell data ethically without selling out its customers |
| **Efficiency financing on measured evidence** | Underwrite equipment upgrades against measured consumption, repay from verified savings | A financial partner, a regulatory position, and a measurement chain trustworthy enough to settle money against. **This is where accuracy stops being a spec and becomes a contract** |
| **Demand-response participation** | Aggregate many small sites into a dispatchable response resource | Market structures that admit small commercial aggregation, plus control capability — which reverses F6 |
| **Utility-side deployment** | The device becomes infrastructure rather than a consumer product | A utility procurement relationship, IS 16444-class certification, and a completely different company shape |
| **Physics-informed models on national scale** | Constrain learned models with known electrical relationships across a very large fleet | The fleet, the research capability, and a reason beyond elegance |
| **Open measurement standard** | Publish the schema and let others build on it | A position secure enough that openness is a moat rather than a giveaway |
| **Sub-₹2,000 monitor** | Price point that changes who can buy one | A fundamentally different sensing approach — the 9-CT stack alone exceeds this today |

## Two ideas that sound like moonshots but are actually rejections

These get proposed repeatedly. They are not parked; they are **decided against**, and the reasons do not expire:

**Appliance lifetime prediction from current signature alone.** The physics does not support the claim. Current draw tells you what a motor is doing today, not how many bearing revolutions remain. Selling this would be selling a guess with a progress bar.

**Revenue-grade metering / billing authority.** A different product, a different regulatory regime, a different certification chain, and a different company. Pursuing it would mean abandoning the retrofit, non-invasive, behind-the-meter position that makes V1 buildable by 3–5 people.

---

## The discipline

Every idea above becomes conceivable only because devices are measuring real circuits. **None of them is a reason to change what V1 is.**

> Measure before you claim. Validate before you build. Park anything that does not ship V1.
